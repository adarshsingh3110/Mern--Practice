RECIPE OF MAGGIE

INGREDIENTS:

  • 1 packet Maggi noodles
  • 1½ cups water
  • Tastemaker masala (comes inside the packet)

RECIPE:

  1. Boil 1½ cups of water in a pan.
  2. Add the Maggi noodles cake into the boiling water
  3. Add the tastemaker masala that comes inside the packet
  4. Cook for 2–3 minutes while stirring gently.
  5. When the water thickens and noodles become soft, turn off the gas and serve hot
